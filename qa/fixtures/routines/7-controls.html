Platform Intern, Formwise

Location: Toronto, ON. Fall 2027, 16 weeks. Open to students in Canada.

Formwise builds the form engine behind thousands of online applications. As a Platform Intern you will work on the rendering layer that turns a schema into accessible fields, and on the tooling that checks every form we ship for keyboard and screen reader support.

You will pair with an engineer on the forms team for the whole term, write tests for the components you touch, and present one change you shipped at the end of the term. Past interns have rebuilt our date picker, added a phone field that formats as you type, and cut our bundle size by a third.

What we look for: comfort with one programming language, curiosity about how browsers expose pages to assistive technology, and care for details. No prior internship is needed. Students in any year of a computer science, engineering or design program are welcome.

How we work: small teams, short reviews, and a weekly demo. The office is a short walk from Union Station, and the team is in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. The rest of the week is remote within Canada.

Pay and benefits: an hourly rate set by your year of study, a transit pass, and a learning budget. We are an equal opportunity employer and welcome applicants of every background.
